Privacy Policy
Effective November 23, 2026

Daywell is designed to work without a Daywell account, advertising, analytics, or tracking. The developer does not operate a server that receives your Habit data.

Data stored on your devices
Daywell stores Habits, their occurrences, and settings in the app’s local container.

iCloud
When iCloud is enabled for Daywell, the app uses Apple CloudKit to synchronize your habits, their occurrences, and settings through your private iCloud database.
You can manage Daywell's access to iCloud through your device's iCloud settings.
Apple processes iCloud data under its own terms and privacy policy. The developer does not use this data for advertising, analytics, or profiling.

Data collection
Daywell contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs. The developer does not receive your Habit data through the app and does not sell personal information.

Retention and deletion
Daywell stores your data locally on your device and, when iCloud synchronization is enabled, in your private iCloud database.
Deleting Daywell from your device removes its local app data but does not necessarily delete data stored in iCloud or on your other devices.
You can manage data stored in iCloud through your Apple device's iCloud storage settings.
The developer does not have direct access to your private iCloud database and cannot remotely delete its contents on your behalf.

Support
Information you choose to send when requesting support is used only to respond to that request. Do not send Habit information or other sensitive information unless it is necessary to resolve your request. For support-related inquiries please email ruslangazizovwork@gmail.com.

Changes
This policy may be updated when Daywell’s data practices change. The effective date above will be updated with the policy.
